# Chat PWA — One Home, Three Rooms

With **ntfybridge**, turn your Jeedom installation into a private messaging app accessible from your phone, tablet or computer.

In this example, three users share the same interface:

- **Dad**: administers Jeedom and monitors the house.
- **Mams**: uses home automation commands and the family chat.
- **Lea**: chats with her parents and views authorized information.

<img src="/img/plugins/ntfybridge/users.png" alt="Users example" style={{maxWidth: '380px', width: '100%', borderRadius: '12px', display: 'block', margin: '1rem auto'}} />

They have three rooms: **🏠 Home**, **🖥️ Jeedom** and **💬 Family**.

---

## 🏠 Home Room — Talking to Jeedom

In the ntfybridge equipment configuration, two topics are defined: the **listen topic** (on which Jeedom receives your commands) and the **response topic** (on which Jeedom sends its replies). In Family Chat, this translates into two distinct rooms: you send your messages in the room linked to the listen topic, and replies arrive in the room linked to the response topic.

:::tip Response template example with binary mapping
```
🏠 **Light Status**

💡 **Living Room**: {#[Matter][Living Room][State]#|0:⚫ Off|1:🟡 On#}

💡 **Kitchen**: {#[Matter][Kitchen][State]#|0:⚫ Off|1:🟡 On#}

💡 **Bedroom**: {#[Matter][Bedroom][State]#|0:⚫ Off|1:🟡 On#}

──────────────

🏡 *Status sent by Jeedom*
```
The `{#[Object][Equipment][Command]#|0:text|1:text#}` syntax inserts the value of a Jeedom command with a readable label based on its binary state.
:::

:::info Multiple keywords per rule
Each rule accepts multiple comma-separated keywords — Jeedom responds or acts as soon as one of them is recognized in the message.

Example: `lights, light status, !lights` triggers the same rule regardless of the phrasing.
:::

:::info Confirmation button
For sensitive actions (open the gate, disarm the alarm…), enable the **Confirmation** option in the rule. Jeedom sends a notification with **Yes ✓ / No ✗** buttons before executing anything. The token expires after the configured timeout (default: 60 s).
:::

## 📱 A PWA Installed on the Phone

The chat can be added to the home screen of an iPhone or Android device, like a native app.

Each family member gets:

- Their rooms and conversations.
- Push notifications for the rooms they subscribe to.
- Message, image and file sending.
- Rooms accessible according to their ntfy permissions.

Conversations remain hosted on your own server.

---

## 🔐 Private Access with Tailscale

For a fully private setup, the chat can be accessed via **Tailscale** — without exposing Jeedom on the internet.

HTTPS and Web Push notifications are handled automatically by `tailscale serve`. See the [getting started guide →](setup.mdx) for configuration.

## Three Use Cases, One Interface

| Room | Use |
|---|---|
| 🏠 **Home** | Interact with home automation — commands, questions, confirmations |
| 🖥️ **Jeedom** | Receive alerts and monitor services |
| 💬 **Family** | Chat, share photos and files |

With ntfybridge, the same PWA becomes both a family messenger and a communication interface with your connected home.
